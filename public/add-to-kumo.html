Add to Kumo

1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:

Add to Kumo

2a. Many items at once (fastest):

  1. Search on mobile.yangkeduo.com (Pinduoduo), e.g. "遥控车" or "盲盒手办". Scroll down to load more items.
  2. Click the Add to Kumo bookmark. All items from the search show in a grid.
  3. Tap the items you like, then Save selected. Photos, English name, price and sizes are filled in automatically.

2b. One item (to pick photos and name yourself):

  1. Open the item's page on Pinduoduo and click Add to Kumo.
  2. Tap the photos to use (one item, no text photos), check the name, click Save.

New items show on the site after a page refresh. To fix a name, price or photo, or to delete an item, change its row in the "Kumo Products" Google Sheet.
